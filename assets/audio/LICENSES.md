authors ask for credit as a courtesy, so the game credits page should say "Sound effects by Kenney (kenney.nl)"
and "Ambient track by yd (opengameart.org)".

Nothing here comes from StarCraft or any other franchise.

## Notes

- The OpenGameArt page for "Background space track" is tagged loop, drone and space. It is about 4.5 MB, which is
  several minutes long. It is not a short loop, and nobody has checked that its ends meet seamlessly. Check it in
  the game before relying on it as a loop.
- Kenney's license texts are in each pack's `License.txt`, which was not kept. The CC0 terms are stated above.
